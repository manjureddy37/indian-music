OUR HERITAGE

A Rich Musical Tradition

Indian Classical Music has a long and rich cultural history. Music has traditionally been connected with spirituality, culture, storytelling and artistic expression.

Indian classical music is primarily represented by two major traditions: Hindustani Classical Music of North India and Carnatic Classical Music of South India.

Both traditions place great importance on melody, rhythm, improvisation and the relationship between the performer and the audience.

TWO TRADITIONS

Hindustani & Carnatic Music

Hindustani Classical Music

Hindustani music is the classical music tradition associated mainly with North India. It features ragas, improvisation and a wide range of vocal and instrumental styles.

Carnatic Classical Music

Carnatic music is the classical music tradition of South India. It is known for structured compositions, intricate rhythms and devotional musical traditions.

UNDERSTAND

Important Concepts

Raga

A melodic framework used to create musical compositions and improvisations.

Tala

A rhythmic cycle that provides the rhythmic structure of a musical performance.

Swara

Musical notes that form the foundation of melody in Indian classical music.
