⬆️

The ascending movement of notes in a raga.

⬇️

The descending movement of notes in a raga.

Why Are Ragas Important?

🎵 Melodic Identity

A raga provides a distinctive framework that guides the melodic development of a classical performance.

🎼 Improvisation

Ragas provide musicians with a framework within which they can explore and improvise creatively.

🕰️ Time & Tradition

Some raga traditions associate particular ragas with specific times of day or seasons.

🌺 Cultural Heritage

Ragas are an important part of India's musical heritage and continue to be performed and taught today.
