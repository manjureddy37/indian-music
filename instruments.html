Famous Indian Instruments

Indian Classical Music uses a wide variety of traditional instruments. Each instrument has its own unique sound, history and role in performance.

Sitar

A famous string instrument strongly associated with Hindustani classical music. It is known for its rich and resonant sound.

Veena

An important string instrument in Carnatic music, traditionally associated with South Indian classical music.

Tabla

A pair of hand drums widely used in Hindustani classical music for providing rhythmic accompaniment.

Mridangam

A major percussion instrument used in Carnatic music and commonly played during classical performances.

Flute

A wind instrument made traditionally from bamboo and known for its soft and expressive musical tone.

Violin

The violin has become an important instrument in Carnatic music and is commonly used to accompany vocal performances.

CLASSIFICATION

Types of Instruments

String Instruments

Instruments such as Sitar, Veena and Sarod produce sound through vibrating strings.

Percussion Instruments

Instruments such as Tabla and Mridangam provide rhythm and beats.

Wind Instruments

Instruments such as Flute and Nadaswaram create melodies using air.
