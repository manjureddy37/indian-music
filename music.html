CLASSICAL TRADITIONS

Hindustani & Carnatic

Indian Classical Music has two major traditions. Each has its own styles, compositions, ragas and approaches to rhythm and performance.

Hindustani Classical Music

The classical music tradition of North India. It is known for improvisation, melodic exploration and expressive performances.

  • 🎵 Dhrupad
  • 🎵 Khayal
  • 🎵 Thumri
  • 🎵 Dadra

Carnatic Classical Music

The classical music tradition of South India. It is known for structured compositions, complex rhythms and devotional music.

  • 🎵 Kriti
  • 🎵 Varnam
  • 🎵 Tillana
  • 🎵 Ragam-Tanam-Pallavi
